Projects

● Live

Steel Signal

Game · 2026 · Three.js · strategy · drones + EW

A turn-based hex wargame for the drone age — electronic warfare, strike drones and fragile supply lines.

Why I made it

Panzer Corps taught me to love hexes; the drone war rewrote the rules. Nobody had built the sequel that understands both, so I did.

● Live

BEREM

Game · 2026 · kids · reading · offline

A Slovene reading game for first grade — nine modes from missing letters to dinosaurs and car brands.

Why I made it

My first-grader was learning to read. The levels follow his school syllables, and the reward sounds are his own recorded voice.

● Live

Front Office

Game · 2026 · React · cards · basketball

A basketball GM dynasty card game — draft, trade and coach a Slovenian club through the seasons.

Why I made it

Basketball plus spreadsheets is my comfort food. I wanted the GM fantasy without the 4 GB install.

Zadnji spust

Game · 2026 · pixel art · adventure · procedural music

A cinematic pixel-art adventure in two acts — The Dig meets Foundation, with a shooter interlude.

Why I made it

I grew up on LucasArts. This is my love letter: slow scenes, a radio voice, and music that knows when to go quiet.

  • ● Live

    Neon City Loop

    Website · 2026 · animation · art

    A one-page gallery for a single animated cyberpunk frame — searchlights, window flicker, billboards.

    Why I made it

  • Minas Tirith

    Website · 2026 · Blender · 3D · scrollytelling

    A scrollytelling diorama of the White City, rebuilt phase by phase in Blender.

    Why I made it

    I wanted to watch a city grow the way a chronicle reads — five phases, one slow scroll.

  • The Long Front

    Game · 2026 · TypeScript · grand strategy · canvas

    Grand strategy on the Russia–Ukraine war in the shape of Hearts of Iron: continuous time you pause, 66 real regions, and a front line the simulation draws rather than a designer.

    Why I made it

    I kept reading about a war fought with drones, shells and factories, and wanted to understand it from the inside — where cutting one road starves a salient without a shot fired.

  • ● Live

    Senbon Torii

    Website · 2026 · Three.js · 3D walk

    A first-person walk through a thousand vermilion gates, with light that shifts as you go.

    Why I made it

    Fushimi Inari without the plane ticket. Walking meditation, rendered.

  • OXA SpO₂

    Website · 2026 · concept · health

    Concept page for a sports SpO₂ wristband — 'every breath counts', from track to sleep.

    Why I made it

    Exploring what a health wearable's story should feel like when the stakes are personal.

  • Vidim cilj

    Website · 2026 · charity · accessibility · WCAG

    Site for a charity running free sport programmes for blind and visually impaired people since 2011.

    Why I made it

    For athletes who can't see the finish line and aim for it anyway. Contrast, text size and focus order are the whole point here, not a checkbox.

  • ● Live

    Raketa nad mestom

    Game · 2026 · arcade · kids

    A pixel arcade rocket for two brothers — dodge, land, refuel, brag.

    Why I made it

    My sons asked for a rocket game over breakfast. Version two shipped by the weekend.
